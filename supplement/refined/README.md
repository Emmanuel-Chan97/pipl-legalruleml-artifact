# Source-anchored role profile for the PIPL pilot

This second-stage regression exercise compares six normative roles at Article 21(2) and Article 57(1)–(2) against explicit project expectations. It was designed with knowledge of the original five mutations. Its results measure those examples only. The expectations express the project author's selected reading of the specified statutory text. No legal expert independently annotated these tests.

## Run

Place this folder beside `replication/` (or directly inside it), with Python 3.9+ and `lxml` installed. Run `python3 run_refined.py` from this directory. The command writes CSV to standard output. `refined_observed.csv` records the run for this draft. No network or reasoning service is required.

The runner verifies the fixed XML digest, verifies the nine source intervals in the declarations against the corresponding 2021 article strings in the bundled JSON export, and imports the original eight mutation/control cases and validators from `../replication/`. The last six rows are supplemental, locally authored probes. The CSV keeps XML schema conformance, reference resolution, the original fixed pilot, and the second-stage check separate.

## What is compared

- `expectations.json` contains nine Chinese excerpts, the publication date and source URL, six rule roles, typed modalities, act and guard formulae, rule-local role/bearer bindings, two outgoing edge expectations, and notes on the reading. Code-point intervals are zero-based and half-open in the stored article strings, including their heading and formatting characters. They are not offsets into the publisher's website.
- `refined.py` resolves each version-linked Chinese paragraph citation through the XML's LegalReference and Association elements. It identifies rule candidates by an action predicate within that paragraph. When two rules in Article 57(2) concern personal notice, it selects them using mitigation or department competence in the antecedent. These selectors do not test for the changed waiver modality, the validity-of-order predicate, the legal atom's negation or the priority pointers.
- An implementation-independent paraphrase of the two outgoing priority constraints is “the waiver defeats only ordinary personal notice” and “the duty triggered by exercise of the department’s power defeats the waiver.” Actual pointer endpoints are obtained by reading the discovered statement keys. No statement identifier occurs in the declarations or the checker. The mutation scripts necessarily address particular nodes in the fixed fixture.
- XML variables are matched under a one-to-one local renaming. Argument positions carry legal roles, and the same role must bind consistently from the guard through the action and bearer. The order rule checks incident, affected person, competent department and valid order for the same department, processor, person and incident across the relevant predicates. It makes no determination of whether an order in the world is legally valid.
- The payload grammar used here contains atoms over variables, `And`, `Or` and `Neg`. It compares exact formula shape while disregarding order of immediate children of `And` and `Or`. Expected sibling skeletons have distinct predicates. The checker does not establish logical equivalence between arbitrary fragments and does not process every permitted LegalRuleML serialization.
- Local labels in the declarations are role variables: `c` processor, `t` entrusted party, `k` contract, `x` information, `e` incident, `d` department, `p` affected person. Bare strings in formula declarations give an atom's project predicate followed by its role arguments; arrays give an operator followed by its children. Guard names and predicate names are stable within this profile. A replacement ontology would need an explicit mapping.

## Results and boundaries

The five original altered legal readings pass the Compact XSD and link checks. All five are flagged by the new profile, including the two missed by the original fixed pilot. A consistent statement rename now passes. So do a simple operand permutation, simultaneous opaque relabeling of all 124 statement keys plus operand reversal, and uniform renaming of variables in the reconstructed order rule. The new profile still flags the semantics-preserving regrouping of the order guard's positive conjuncts. Altering the distinct remedy obligation lies outside the six declarations and goes undetected. Mismatching the addressees of the order and the resulting notice is detected. These are finite diagnostics and no population accuracy is estimated.

The trace for the ordered-notice rule is deliberately qualified. “有权要求个人信息处理者通知个人” supports a power to order notice. Conditioning a downstream duty on its valid exercise, and reinstating personal notice notwithstanding mitigation, are the project's reconstructions. Similarly, choosing an explicit override for the permission is a modeling decision. The checker tests adherence to those choices. It does not infer them from the Chinese excerpts or adjudicate rival interpretations.

Paragraph citations and candidate predicates establish the domain for each role. An absent or ambiguous required candidate produces a diagnostic. Other rules, additional incoming edges from unrelated rules, unmodeled fields and evidence-method labels are outside the implemented contracts. Mapping to the expected source URL and citation plus checking the saved Chinese text do not establish that a source URI currently resolves or that every evidence association is correct.
