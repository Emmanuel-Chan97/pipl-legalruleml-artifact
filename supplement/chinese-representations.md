# Chinese-law comparisons

Targeted public search on 23 November 2026. We sought a report or artifact explicitly applying LegalRuleML to the enacted PIPL, and related work on directed scope or rules in mainland Chinese statutes. This was an open-web and research-literature search, not a survey of all implementations or subscription-only Chinese databases. Terms included ‘"LegalRuleML" "PIPL"’, ‘"LegalRuleML" "personal information protection law"’, ‘"LegalRuleML" "Chinese law"’, ‘"LegalRuleML" "Chinese Civil Code"’, ‘"LegalRuleML" "Chinese legal"’, ‘"LegalRuleML" 中国 法律 形式化 建模 民法典 个人信息保护法’ and ‘元法 一阶逻辑 民法典’. Sectoral hits and primary articles were checked for the jurisdiction, representation and evaluated task. We did not verify an earlier public native LegalRuleML encoding of the PIPL in these results. This bounded observation does not establish priority or exclude proprietary work.
